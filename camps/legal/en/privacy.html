1. Who looks after your data

This policy explains how the information system of the English Camp, Faculty of Education (9th edition) collects, uses and discloses your personal data, in line with Thailand’s Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019) (PDPA).

  • Data controller: the English Education Program, Faculty of Education, Loei Rajabhat University (the Organiser), which decides the purposes and means of use.
  • Developer and operator: sorasukt.com / STNET World Solutions carries out technical work on the Organiser’s instructions as data processor.
  • Contact on personal data: legal@sorasukt.com

3. Legal bases we rely on

  • Performance of the arrangement and running the activities: student data, department selection, shirt designs, lucky draw, QR check.
  • Legitimate interests: activity logs, system security, preventing rule-breaking (for example the AI-image check and the location check).
  • Explicit consent: health and food details, which are sensitive data under section 26. Before saving them you must tick the consent box; the System stores the time and the policy version you consented to. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing every field of that group on the “Personal information” page, or by telling legal@sorasukt.com. Withdrawal does not affect use already made.
  • Legal obligation: where law or a public authority requires it.

4. Who we disclose data to

  • Other participants and the public: only the designer name, class group and picture of a shirt design on the shirt gallery page (when an admin opens it). Nickname/AKA may appear on name tags or camp activities. Other data is not shown to other students.
  • Admins: each admin’s permissions are split by duty and set by the system owner, with a log of permission changes. Health and food details can be opened only by admins granted the “view and export health details” permission, which is not given by default; they are not shown in the student list, an admin must open them student by student or export them, and every view or export is logged with who and when. These details exist only to arrange food and look after safety. Deleting a student and erasing expired data also need their own permissions.
  • Staff who are not admins (for example QR scanners) see only what their duty needs and never health details.
  • Technical service providers: Auth0 (identity), Google (sign-in account), Cloudflare (web, database, files), Sightengine (AI-image check; receives the submitted shirt picture), GitHub (source code and deployment; no user data). Details are in Third parties.
  • By law: when ordered by a court or competent authority.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

5. Automated decision: AI-image check

When you submit a shirt design the System checks it automatically in two ways: tags embedded in the file (for example AI-tool metadata) and Sightengine’s image-analysis service, which returns a probability score that the image is AI-generated.

  • Effect on you: if the System is highly confident the image is AI-generated, the design is not accepted and your submissions are suspended for 24 hours. If it is suspicious, the design is flagged for an admin, who may exclude it after re-checking.
  • Limits: no detector is 100% accurate; a design you made yourself may be misjudged. A failing service never blocks a student.
  • Right to contest: you may ask a person to review the result by emailing legal@sorasukt.com with your student ID and evidence that you made the design (for example the source file). The Organiser makes the final decision.

6. Transfers abroad

Some of the providers above (for example Cloudflare, Auth0/Okta, Google, Sightengine) may process or store data outside Thailand. We choose providers with protection measures to international standards and use data only as needed for that service.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access and obtain a copy of your data, to have it corrected, erased or de-identified, to restrict or object to its use, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to ask for human review of an automated decision.

  • Do it yourself: on the “Personal information” page you can edit or clear most data, including health and food details.
  • By email: for data you cannot edit (ID, year, gender), erasure, or any other right, send your request to legal@sorasukt.com with your name and student ID. The Organiser will act within the time the law sets.
  • Complaints: if you believe use of your data is unlawful you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Committee Office of Thailand.

9. Data security

  • Connections use HTTPS and sign-in uses a verified university account.
  • Permissions are separated by role; non-admins cannot view other people’s data.
  • Admin permissions are split by duty; sensitive data, deletion and permission management need a specific grant from the system owner.
  • Health details are kept out of the general list and export; only admins with the permission can open them, and each access is logged.
  • The personal-information page hides the national ID, phone numbers and medical conditions until you reveal them.
  • Important admin actions are logged for later audit.

Despite our efforts no system is 100% secure. If you notice anything unusual or a leak, tell legal@sorasukt.com at once; the Organiser will give notice as the law requires.

10. Cookies and browser storage

The site uses no advertising or tracking cookies. It uses browser storage (sessionStorage / localStorage) to keep you signed in, remember choices and run the pages. Auth0 may set cookies needed for sign-in. You can clear these in your browser settings but may need to sign in again.

Fonts and scripts are loaded from our own server, so your IP address is not sent to a font provider or external CDN. Camera access (QR scanning) and location (registration-area check) are requested through your browser when needed; camera images are read on your device and are not uploaded.

11. Users under 20

The guardian’s phone number you enter is used for emergency contact only. Please tell your guardian that you provided it. If a user is a minor who legally needs a guardian’s consent, the Organiser will act as appropriate.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will show the last-updated date at the top. When a new version changes something material, students are asked to accept again, and to re-consent to health data if the purpose changes.
